Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 | Effective for all AMPEdge users & mobile applications

Summary: At AMPEdge Solutions, your privacy and trust are our highest priorities. We collect only the information strictly required to dispatch certified electrician partners to your location, deliver genuine marketplace electrical components, and provide 24/7 technical customer support. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AMPEdge Solutions ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses your personal data when you visit our website (https://ampedge.co.in) or utilize our mobile applications and electrical on-demand booking services in India.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Personal Contact Information: Name, phone number, email address, physical service address, pin code, and city provided during service booking or marketplace orders.
  • Geolocation Data: Precise or approximate GPS coordinates provided with your permission to identify and dispatch the closest verified electrician partner and accurately calculate travel time/distance.
  • Booking & Transaction Details: Type of electrical service booked, parts ordered, preferred service time slot, transaction IDs, payment mode (UPI, Net Banking, Card), and order invoices. We do not store sensitive credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, device model, operating system version, browser type, app diagnostics, and crash logs to improve app performance and user experience.
  • Customer Support Communications: Audio/text chat logs from our AI Support agent, WhatsApp inquiries, support tickets, and feedback forms.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To process service bookings and assign verified electrician technicians within your locality.
  • To pack, dispatch, and deliver electrical hardware and solar products ordered via our Marketplace.
  • To verify and activate our official 90-Day Post-Service Warranty on all repairs and installations.
  • To send critical transaction updates, OTPs, appointment confirmations, and service invoices via SMS, WhatsApp, and Email.
  • To prevent fraudulent bookings, unauthorized transactions, and maintain platform security.

4. Mobile App Permissions & Disclosures (Google Play Compliant)

When using the AMPEdge Mobile App on Android devices, we may request the following permissions:

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Required to detect your service address, estimate technician ETA, and calculate local travel charges. Location is only accessed while the app is active or when you explicitly share your coordinates.
  • Camera & Photo Storage (CAMERA / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Optional permission requested only when you upload pictures of damaged electrical switchboards, wiring faults, or solar rooftops for accurate quote estimates.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): To notify you when a technician is dispatched, has arrived, or when an order status updates.
  • Phone Call (CALL_PHONE): To enable direct calling with your assigned technician or support desk.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with trusted third-party providers to deliver our services safely:

  • Razorpay: Encrypted payment gateway processing compliant with RBI and PCI-DSS standards.
  • Google Maps & Location Services: Mapping, address geocoding, and distance calculation.
  • WhatsApp Business API: Automated real-time customer updates and booking alerts.

6. Data Security & Storage

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is secured using industry-standard 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption. Data is stored on secure cloud servers with restricted role-based access controls and regular security audits.

7. User Rights & Data Deletion Policy

You have the right to access, rectify, or request the complete deletion of your personal account data at any time:

  • To request account deletion or data erasure, send an email with your registered phone number to ampedge.info@gmail.com with the subject line "Account & Data Deletion Request".
  • Our compliance team will verify and permanently purge your customer record, saved addresses, and active session tokens within 7 business days, retaining only legally required tax/invoice archives.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for individuals who are at least 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
