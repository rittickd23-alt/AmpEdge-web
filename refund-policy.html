Refund & Cancellation

Clear, customer-first return and refund guidelines.

1. Marketplace Product Returns (7-Day Policy)

We want you to be completely satisfied with your electrical hardware purchases. If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product:

  • You may initiate a return within 7 days from the delivery date.
  • Items must be unused, unmounted, and in their original packaging with all seals and accessories intact.
  • Our pickup partner will inspect the product upon collection, and your replacement or full refund will be processed immediately.

2. Service Booking Cancellation

  • Free Cancellation: You can cancel any scheduled service appointment at zero cost up to 2 hours before the technician's scheduled arrival time.
  • Late Cancellation: Cancellations made after the technician is already en route or within 2 hours of the slot may incur a nominal dispatch charge of ₹99.

3. 90-Day Post-Service Warranty Claims

If an electrical issue recurs within 90 days of an AMPEdge service, we will dispatch an expert senior technician to inspect and fix the issue completely free of cost.

4. Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method (Bank Account, UPI, or Credit/Debit Card) within 5 to 7 business days.
